Privacy

Triathlon coach is a personal app with one user, its owner, who uses it to plan his own training.

With his permission it reads his Google calendars to find free time, and writes training sessions to one calendar of his. Calendar data is used only to plan that training, partly with the Anthropic API, and is kept on his own server and in his own accounts.

Nothing is shared with, sold to or used for anyone else. Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account's security settings.
